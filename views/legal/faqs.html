Why won't Stripe accept my phone number at checkout?

Payment gateways may not allow new phone numbers, especially from low cost or pre-pay carriers, so it is best to not select save information for next time on Stripe or use a mobile phone from a contract carrier.

All bookings are billed strictly in Australian dollars (AUD$) inclusive of {{gst_percent}}% Australian GST. Leaving “Save my information for faster checkout” unchecked on Stripe lets you pay directly with your card without phone verification.

Standard online bookings run Monday to Friday at our {{pickup_label}} depot: pickup is 9:00 AM – 10:00 AM and drop-off (next day or final return day) is 8:00 AM – 9:00 AM.

Need an earlier pickup or drop-off time? That is fine — just message admin in advance. Saturday and Sunday pickups or drop-offs also require you to message admin first before booking.

What driver licence is required and how is it verified?

Drivers must be at least {{min_age}} years old with a current, full Australian driver licence permitting manual vehicle driving (our Renault Kangoo MAXI is a 6-speed manual). Learner licences are not accepted, and provisional licences are reviewed by our team.

During registration you upload both the front and back of your driver licence. Our automated check uses Google Gemini to extract licence details (uploaded images are processed in memory and are not stored). See our AI Policy (ISO/IEC 42001) and Privacy Policy for full details.
