Responsible AI

AI Policy (ISO/IEC 42001)

Last updated 30 October 2026

This policy explains how {{business_name}} (operated by AI Foundation) uses artificial intelligence, and the controls we apply. It is structured around ISO/IEC 42001:2023, the international standard for AI management systems. This policy describes how we work with the standard; it is not a claim of certification.

1. Our commitments

  • Purpose-limited: we use AI only where it clearly helps customers, and never to make pricing or marketing decisions about individuals.
  • Transparent: we tell you when AI is used, what it does and what happens to your data.
  • Human oversight: a person reviews any case the AI can't confirm, and you can always ask for a human review.
  • Privacy by design: we send the AI only what it needs and keep nothing we don't need.
  • Accountable: AI Foundation's leadership owns this policy and reviews it at least once a year.

2. AI systems we use

Driver licence verification

  • What it does: reads the photo of the front of your licence (name, date of birth, licence number, expiry, state, licence type) and checks it matches the details you entered. It also flags photos that are unclear or look altered.
  • Model and provider: Claude, a large language model by Anthropic, accessed through Anthropic's commercial API.
  • Data: only the licence photo and a fixed set of instructions are sent. The photo is held in memory, sent once, and then discarded. We never store the image. We keep the extracted licence details and the outcome.
  • Consent: you agree to this check when you register. If you'd rather not use it, contact us and we'll arrange a manual check.

3. How decisions are made

  • Verified automatically: only when every detail matches, the licence is current and nothing looks unusual.
  • Sent to a person: provisional or probationary licences, suspected alterations, unreadable results, or when the AI service is unavailable.
  • Not verified: when the photo isn't a licence, is too blurry, is a learner licence, has expired, or the details don't match. You're told why and can upload again or message us for a human review. No adverse decision is final without the option of review by a person.

4. Risk management

We assessed the risks of this system before using it, including:

  • Errors in reading a licence: mitigated by strict matching, human review of uncertain cases, and a right to appeal.
  • Unfair outcomes: the check compares facts printed on the card with what you entered. It doesn't assess appearance or infer sensitive characteristics, and we monitor rejection rates and reasons for patterns.
  • Privacy: image never stored; minimum data sent; provider bound by commercial data protection terms.
  • Availability: if the AI service fails, the system defaults to manual review rather than rejecting customers.
  • Misuse: uploads are rate-limited and every check is recorded in our audit log (without the image).

5. Monitoring and improvement

Staff review the audit log and any customer appeals regularly. We record incidents involving AI errors, investigate root causes, and update the system or this policy as needed. Changes to the AI model or its instructions are tested before release.

6. Questions or concerns

If you think an AI decision about you was wrong, or you have a question about how we use AI, message us from your account or contact us {{contact_email}}. A person will review it.
