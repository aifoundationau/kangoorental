{{business_name}}, operated by AI Foundation ("we", "us"), follows the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs). This policy explains what personal information we collect, why, and how we look after it.

1. What we collect

  • Identity and contact details: full name, date of birth, email, mobile number and residential address.
  • Driver licence details: licence number, issuing state, expiry date and class, and the result of our licence check.
  • Location when you book: with your permission, your device's location is checked each time you book to confirm you're within {{radius_km}} km of our {{pickup_label}} depot. We keep only the distance with the booking, not your coordinates. (Accounts created before 31 October 2026 also hold the location given at registration.)
  • Location panel: the bar at the bottom of each page shows your closest suburb, local time and weather. All prices on this site are in Australian dollars (AUD). The panel uses your approximate location from your internet connection, your device's location if you choose to share it, or a suburb you pick. This is saved in your browser only, not in your account.
  • Insurance details: insurer, policy number, policy expiry and your confirmation of indemnity cover.
  • Bookings and payments: booking times, AUD prices, payments and refunds. Card details are entered on Stripe's secure page and are never seen or stored by us.
  • Messages you send us through your account, and security logs (such as sign-in times and IP addresses).

2. Your licence photos are processed in memory and extracted details are stored

When you upload photos of the front and back of your driver licence, they are held in our server's memory only and sent once to Google Gemini AI to extract all printed licence details (such as name, DOB, licence number, card number, authority, classes, conditions, issue/expiry dates, addresses, and security checks) and compare them with what you entered. The image buffers are then discarded immediately — we never save raw photos to disk or backups. We store the extracted structured licence information on your customer profile in our database so our Superadmin and Admin team can verify eligibility and view customer profiles in the Admin panel. See our AI Policy for how this check works.

3. Why we collect it

  • to confirm your identity, age, licence and eligibility to hire;
  • to take and manage bookings, payments and refunds;
  • to contact you about your bookings and reply to your messages;
  • to meet legal obligations, such as tax record-keeping and responding to infringement notices and lawful requests; and
  • to protect our customers, vehicle and systems from fraud and misuse.

We don't sell your personal information or use it for advertising.

4. Who we share it with

We use trusted service providers to run the service. They may store or process information outside Australia:

  • Google Cloud (Firestore) stores our customer database in the United States (nam5 multi-region).
  • Google Gemini API reads uploaded front and back driver licence photos in memory to extract structured licence fields. Processing may take place in the United States.
  • Stripe processes AUD booking payments (United States and other countries).
  • OpenStreetMap (Nominatim) and Open-Meteo receive location coordinates, rounded to about 100 m and without your name or account details, to look up your suburb and local weather for the location panel.
  • Hosting and email providers we use to operate the website.

We take reasonable steps under APP 8 to make sure these providers protect your information to a standard consistent with the APPs. We may also disclose information where the law requires it, for example to police or a road authority.

5. How long we keep it

We keep account details while your account is open. All messages exchanged between customers and our admin team are stored for 7 years, including after an account is deleted by a superadmin (with profile details anonymised). Booking and payment records are kept for at least five years (and 7 years where linked to financial ledgers) to meet Australian tax law. When a superadmin deletes an account upon request, we remove personal profile details and keep only the archived messages and anonymised records we're required to retain.

6. Security

We protect your information with encryption in transit and at rest, hashed passwords, role-based staff access and audit logging. See our Security Policy. If a data breach is likely to cause you serious harm, we'll notify you and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) under the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme.

You can see and update most of your details in your account. Accounts cannot be self-deleted; account deletion is carried out exclusively by a superadmin upon request (message admin from your account or contact us {{contact_email}}). To access or correct other information, contact us {{contact_email}}. We'll respond within 30 days.
