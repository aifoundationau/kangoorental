{{business_name}}, operated by AI Foundation ("we", "us"), follows the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs). This policy explains what personal information we collect, why, and how we look after it.

1. What we collect

  • Identity and contact details: full name, date of birth, email, mobile number and residential address.
  • Driver licence details: licence number, issuing state, expiry date and class, and the result of our licence check.
  • Location at registration: your device's coordinates and distance from our depot, collected once with your permission to confirm you live in our service area (within {{radius_km}} km of our {{pickup_label}} depot).
  • Insurance details: insurer, policy number, policy expiry and your confirmation of indemnity cover.
  • Bookings and payments: booking times, prices, token purchases and balances. Card details are entered on Stripe's secure page and are never seen or stored by us.
  • Messages you send us through your account, and security logs (such as sign-in times and IP addresses).

2. Your licence photo is not stored

When you upload a photo of your licence, it is held in our server's memory only. It is sent once to our AI provider to read the details and compare them with what you entered, and then discarded. We never save the image to disk, to our database or to backups. We keep only the licence details listed above and whether the check passed. See our AI Policy for how this check works.

3. Why we collect it

  • to confirm your identity, age, licence and eligibility to hire;
  • to take and manage bookings, payments and refunds;
  • to contact you about your bookings and reply to your messages;
  • to meet legal obligations, such as tax record-keeping and responding to infringement notices and lawful requests; and
  • to protect our customers, vehicle and systems from fraud and misuse.

We don't sell your personal information or use it for advertising.

4. Who we share it with

We use trusted service providers to run the service. They may store or process information outside Australia:

  • Google Cloud (Firestore) stores our customer database in the United States (nam5 multi-region).
  • Anthropic provides the AI model that reads your licence photo. Processing takes place in the United States.
  • Stripe processes token payments (United States and other countries).
  • Hosting and email providers we use to operate the website.

We take reasonable steps under APP 8 to make sure these providers protect your information to a standard consistent with the APPs. We may also disclose information where the law requires it, for example to police or a road authority.

5. How long we keep it

We keep account details while your account is open. Booking and payment records are kept for at least five years to meet Australian tax law. When you delete your account, we remove your personal details and keep only the anonymised records we're required to retain.

6. Security

We protect your information with encryption in transit and at rest, hashed passwords, role-based staff access and audit logging. See our Security Policy. If a data breach is likely to cause you serious harm, we'll notify you and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) under the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme.

7. Access, correction and deletion

You can see and update most of your details in your account, and delete your account at any time. To access or correct other information, contact us {{contact_email}}. We'll respond within 30 days.

8. Complaints

If you have a privacy concern, contact us first {{contact_email}} and we'll respond within 30 days. If you're not satisfied, you can complain to the OAIC at oaic.gov.au or on 1300 363 992.
