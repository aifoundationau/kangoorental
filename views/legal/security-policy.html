Information security

Security Policy (ISO/IEC 27001)

Last updated 30 October 2026

This policy sets out how {{business_name}} (operated by AI Foundation) protects the confidentiality, integrity and availability of customer information. It is structured around ISO/IEC 27001:2022 and its Annex A controls. This policy describes how we work with the standard; it is not a claim of certification.

1. Scope and responsibility

This policy covers the {{business_name}} website, its database and the service providers that process customer data. AI Foundation's leadership is responsible for information security, approves this policy and reviews it at least once a year or after any significant change or incident.

2. Data minimisation

  • Licence photos are processed in memory and never stored.
  • Card details are handled by Stripe and never reach our systems.
  • We collect only the information needed to verify drivers and run bookings.

3. Access control

  • Role-based access: customers see only their own data; admins manage bookings, customers and messages; only the superadmin can change pricing, currency settings and staff accounts.
  • Staff accounts are personal, never shared, and disabled promptly when no longer needed.
  • The database is reachable only from our server through a dedicated service account. Browsers never access it directly.

4. Authentication and sessions

  • Passwords are hashed with scrypt and a unique salt. We never store or see plain-text passwords.
  • Sessions use random tokens in secure, HttpOnly, SameSite cookies. Only a hash of the token is stored, and sessions expire after {{session_days}} days.
  • Changing a password signs out all other sessions.
  • Sign-in, registration, upload and messaging are rate-limited to slow down guessing and abuse.

5. Application security

  • All traffic is encrypted with HTTPS (TLS), with HTTP Strict Transport Security.
  • Every state-changing request is protected against cross-site request forgery.
  • A strict Content Security Policy, frame blocking and other security headers reduce the risk of cross-site scripting and clickjacking.
  • All input is validated on the server, and output is escaped before display.
  • Payment confirmations from Stripe are verified by signature, and each payment can be credited only once.

6. Data protection

  • Customer data is stored in Google Cloud Firestore, encrypted at rest by Google.
  • Database backups are scheduled and restores are tested periodically.
  • Personal details are removed when a customer deletes their account; records required by law are retained in anonymised form.

7. Logging and monitoring

Security-relevant events, including sign-ins, failed sign-ins, licence checks, pricing changes, token adjustments and booking changes, are written to an audit log that staff review regularly.

8. Suppliers

We use established providers (Google Cloud, Anthropic, Stripe) with recognised security certifications, and we review their security and privacy terms before use and when they change.

9. Incident response

Suspected incidents are contained, investigated and recorded. Where a breach is likely to cause serious harm, we notify affected customers and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner under the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme.

10. Reporting a vulnerability

If you believe you've found a security issue, please contact us {{contact_email}}. Please don't access other people's data or disrupt the service. We'll acknowledge your report and keep you informed.
