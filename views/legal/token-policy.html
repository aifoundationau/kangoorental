Token Policy

Last updated 31 October 2026

At {{business_name}} you pay with prepaid tokens rather than paying by card each time you book. This policy explains how tokens work. It forms part of our Terms of Service.

1. Buying tokens

  • Each token costs US$1. For example, 100 tokens cost US$100.
  • Our payment provider, Stripe, charges you in US dollars. Your bank may charge a foreign transaction fee.
  • You can use your tokens for our products and services.

2. Spending tokens

  • We're an Australian business, so our prices are in Australian dollars. At checkout, the tokens you spend are converted to Australian dollars at the exchange rate shown before you confirm.
  • We deduct the number of tokens shown when you confirm, even if the exchange rate changes later.
  • Prices and token amounts are always whole numbers, rounded down.

3. Sharing tokens

  • You can share tokens with other {{business_name}} account holders, such as friends and family.
  • Each share costs 1 token, taken from your balance on top of the tokens you share.
  • To share tokens, message us from your account. Tell us how many tokens to share and the email address of the other person's account.

4. Paying out tokens

  • If your account holds more than 100 tokens, you can ask us to pay them out. We need 72 hours' notice.
  • We pay out in Australian dollars, converted at the exchange rate on the day we pay. If your bank account is outside Australia, the payment should reach you in your local currency.
  • You're responsible for all international wire transfer fees and surcharges.
  • To ask for a payout, message us from your account.

5. Your rights

Nothing in this policy limits your rights under the Australian Consumer Law. Questions? Contact us {{contact_email}}.
