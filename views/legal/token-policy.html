Token Policy

Last updated 31 October 2026

At {{business_name}} you pay with prepaid tokens rather than paying by card each time you book. This policy explains how tokens work. It forms part of our Terms of Service.

1. Buying tokens

  • The default token price is 1 token for {{token_price_aud}} (for example, 100 tokens cost AUD$100 inc GST).
  • All invoices are in Australian dollars (AUD$). Our payment provider, Stripe, processes payments and issues tax invoices in Australian dollars.
  • Visitors in Australia and New Zealand see Australian dollars (AUD$) directly. Visitors in all other nations are shown an indicative currency conversion of AUD$1 converted to USD (US$).
  • You can use your tokens for our products and services.

2. Spending tokens

  • We're an Australian business, so our prices and all tax invoices are in Australian dollars (AUD$).
  • The token amount for each product and service is set by administration (defaulting to 1 token per AUD$1) and is displayed clearly before you confirm your booking.
  • We deduct the number of tokens shown when you confirm.
  • Product prices and token amounts are always whole numbers, rounded up.

3. Sharing tokens

  • You can share tokens with other {{business_name}} account holders, such as friends and family.
  • Each share costs {{token_share_fee}} token, taken from your balance on top of the tokens you share.
  • To share tokens, message us from your account. Tell us how many tokens to share and the email address of the other person's account.

4. Paying out tokens

  • If your account holds more than 100 tokens, you can ask us to pay them out. We need 72 hours' notice.
  • We pay out in Australian dollars (AUD$). If your bank account is outside Australia, the payment should reach you in your local currency.
  • You're responsible for all international wire transfer fees and surcharges.
  • To ask for a payout, message us from your account.

5. Your rights

Nothing in this policy limits your rights under the Australian Consumer Law. Questions? Contact us {{contact_email}}.
